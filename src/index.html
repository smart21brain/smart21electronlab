About

          
Smart21 ElectroLab — a circuit and Arduino Uno emulator for studying electronics. The Arduino emulator runs a practical subset of Arduino C++ (variables, arrays, functions, loops, Serial, pinMode, digitalRead/Write, analogRead/Write, delay, millis, tone…). It is a learning tool, not a replacement for real hardware.
